Questions authors ask

Before we begin.

A little clarity before the first conversation can make the process much easier.

Do I need to have a finished book?

No. Marketing can begin at different stages. Early strategy can help with positioning, audience, launch planning and building a stronger foundation before publication.

Can you help with Amazon?

Yes. Amazon optimization can include product-page messaging, keywords, categories, author positioning and other discoverability and conversion considerations.

Do you work with Goodreads?

Yes. Goodreads can be part of a broader reader-discovery strategy, alongside other channels and promotional opportunities that make sense for your book.

Is your work only for new releases?

No. A backlist title can also benefit from a refreshed position, improved presentation, new promotional angles or a renewed visibility plan.

Will you guarantee a specific number of sales?

No responsible marketer can guarantee a particular sales figure. The focus is on building stronger positioning, discoverability, campaigns and reader pathways that support commercial growth.

How do I get started?

Use the contact form above or email directly. Share your book details, your current stage and what you want to achieve. We can then determine the most useful next step.
